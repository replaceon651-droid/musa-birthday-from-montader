Selected Works

Original Artworks by Musa Haithem

01

Sunset Over the Sea

Acrylic on Canvas

A breathtaking seascape capturing the golden hour where the sun meets the ocean. The bold silhouettes of seabirds in flight against the warm gradient sky showcase Musa's mastery of acrylic techniques and his deep connection to nature's most poetic moments.

02

Golden Serenity

Pencil & Mixed Media

An enchanting forest scene bathed in amber light. The delicate pencil work brings life to every tree and ripple on the water, while the two swans add a touch of romance. This piece reveals Musa's exceptional skill in blending detailed sketching with atmospheric warmth.

03

Desert Moon

Acrylic on Canvas

A dramatic nocturnal landscape where crimson dunes meet a luminous full moon. The scattered stars and sweeping brushstrokes create a sense of infinite space and quiet mystery. This work demonstrates Musa's bold use of color and his ability to evoke emotion through contrast and texture.

The Artist

Musa Haithem is a passionate artist working primarily in pencil and acrylic. His work captures the beauty of nature — from golden sunsets to starlit deserts — with a distinctive style that blends realism with emotional depth. Each piece tells a story, inviting the viewer to pause and feel the moment.
